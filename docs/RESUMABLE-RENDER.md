# Resumable delivery render

`DeliveryConverter`, `OfflineRenderer`, and `DeliveredMastering` retain the same arithmetic as their whole calls. Their whole calls drive `begin`, `step`, and `finish`; `DeliveredMastering::beginJob/stepJob/finishJob` also includes preparation. A zero budget neither prepares an object nor reads or writes a frame. `cancelJob` discards the cursor and leaves the borrowed source intact. A new job resets the converter, chain, latency trim, tap position, and drain counters.

The caller owns planar source and output buffers until `finishJob` or `cancelJob`. Tables and planes cannot overlap across source and output. The job holds pointers to the tables; those tables must also remain valid. At equal rates SRC retains the converter's input gate, and the chain processes the copied delivery samples. No clock or thread is used by the render path.

The converter's cursor stores source frames read, emitted SRC frames, the rational latency's rounded trim, non-finite input count, and silence fed during drain. A step spends at most its budget in source frames, including drain silence. It writes only delivered indices in `[0, ceil(N·L/M))`, where `L:M` is the reduced delivery/source rate ratio. The renderer stores its chain cursor, latency, tap cursor, and output position; a step spends at most its budget in chain frames. It writes `out[n] = y[n + D]`, including the part of `y` emitted by its `D`-frame drain. A call with budget zero preserves all cursors and the output.

`DeliveredMastering::stepRender` converts first into the caller's output and then renders that buffer in place. Starting the renderer and closing its drain each consume one separate step unit. A search pass uses the same source and output buffers: SRC restarts from the original source on each pass and feeds its newly available output blocks to the chain. The converted programme is never kept as a third complete PCM allocation. The legacy whole solve still owns its search decisions and meters; making those decisions resumable is the next search task. Input loudness range has no output parameter and retains one temporary delivered programme for its meter call.

`DeliveredMastering::storageFor` checks the complete geometry before a job begins. For `N` source frames, `D = ceil(N·L/M)` delivered frames, and `C` channels, it reports `sourceBytes = 4CN`, `outputBytes = 4CD`, and `workspaceBytes` from the chain, renderer, and converter preparation declarations. `maxLiveBytes = sourceBytes + outputBytes + workspaceBytes`; `largestBlockBytes` conservatively takes the maximum of those three terms. All products and sums are in `uint64_t`, including on wasm32. This is a conservative demand, so a caller can reserve the complete live set before preparation. The component declarations continue to price individual preparations and refused requests.

`felitronics_mastering_resumable_render_tests` compares the saved conversion loop and whole render composition with different step cuts, pauses in source/SRC/drain, zero budgets, cancel/restart, one-frame programmes, and last-frame impulses. The delivered solver suite compares repeated SRC passes with the prior converted-programme composition, including sample bits and measurements.
