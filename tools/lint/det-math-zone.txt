# --- and the tools that PRINT the diffed text. The long-double lint deliberately skips tools/; for this lint

zone  tools/fcore_clips.h  the clips exposure the clips parity step diffs
zone  tools/fcore_stream.h  the streaming surface the stream parity step diffs after every piece
# The wasm entry point itself. It PRINTS the numbers CI diffs (parity.mjs renders its lufs/dbtp as text and the
# step runs a plain `diff`), and until the libm audit it computed one of them with a constant `20.0 * std::log10 (1e-9)`.
# What CI byte-diffs from this binary is its STDOUT — the block energies and the true peak as a LINEAR bit
# pattern; `dbtp` is printed only under --debug and only to stderr. It is in the zone because the numbers it
# prints on the diffed path come from here, not because every getter it exposes is diffed.
